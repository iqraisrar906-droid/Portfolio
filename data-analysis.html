Statistics & AI

Data Analysis Project

An exploration of how statistical thinking and data visualization can turn a dataset into clear, useful findings.

Project overview

This project follows a data analysis process: understanding the data, summarizing important variables, looking for patterns, and communicating observations clearly. AI-assisted tools can support exploration, while statistical reasoning helps interpret the results carefully.

What I worked on

I considered how to organize and explore data, choose useful summaries, and present findings in an understandable way. The focus is on asking clear questions, interpreting patterns responsibly, and explaining what the data can and cannot tell us.

Skills explored

Statistical thinking Data exploration Data visualization AI-assisted analysis

What I learned

This project connects my BS Statistics studies with my interest in AI. It strengthened my understanding of how to communicate data findings clearly and how to use AI as a support for analysis rather than a replacement for careful interpretation.

AI Automation

Workflow Automation

A practical project exploring how AI and automation can make routine workflows more efficient and reduce repetitive manual steps.

Project overview

This project focuses on mapping a repeatable process, identifying tasks that can be automated, and connecting workflow steps so information moves with less manual effort. AI tools can assist with handling and organizing information as part of the workflow.

What I worked on

I explored the workflow from start to finish, considered where automation could help, and organized the steps into a clearer process. The goal was to apply AI automation to a practical task while learning how to make workflows more consistent.

Skills explored

AI tools Workflow design Process automation Problem solving

What I learned

This work helped me understand how to break a process into manageable steps and look for opportunities to automate repetitive tasks. I am continuing to build my skills through hands-on practice and my AI course.
